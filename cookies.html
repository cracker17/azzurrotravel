Cookie Policy

Exactly what we store on your device, and why.

Effective date: July 30, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 30, 2026  ·  Consent version: v1

This Cookie Policy explains how Azzurro Travel, Inc. ("Azzurro Travel," "we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on azzurrotravel.com. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers personal information more broadly.

The short version: this site sets no cookies at all until you tell us what you are comfortable with. If you accept analytics, we use Google Analytics to count visits. We do not run advertising cookies, we do not build profiles, and we do not sell your data.

1. What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. It can be read back on a later page or a later visit, which is what allows a site to remember something about you — a preference, a session, or the fact that you have been here before. "Similar technologies" means things that achieve the same result by other means, such as browser local storage or a tracking pixel. Where this policy says "cookies," it covers all of them.

Cookies set by azzurrotravel.com are first-party cookies. Cookies set by another company whose code runs on our pages — Google, in our case — are third-party cookies. A session cookie disappears when you close your browser; a persistent cookie stays until it expires or you delete it.

2. How We Ask For Your Consent

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive require your prior, informed, freely given consent before any non-essential cookie is placed on your device. We apply that standard to every visitor, wherever you are, rather than only to visitors we detect as European. In practice:

  • Nothing non-essential runs before you choose. The Google Analytics tag is not merely blocked from setting cookies — it is not even downloaded until you accept. Our site loads with Google Consent Mode v2 set to denied by default.
  • Refusing is exactly as easy as accepting. The banner gives "Accept all" and "Reject all" the same size, position and prominence. Nothing is pre-ticked.
  • Ignoring the banner counts as a refusal. If you scroll past it, close the preferences panel or simply leave, analytics stays off. Continuing to browse is never treated as agreement.
  • You can change your mind at any time, using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of every page, or the button below. Withdrawing consent also deletes the analytics cookies that were set while it applied.
  • Access is never conditional on consent. Every page of this site works identically whether you accept or refuse. There is no cookie wall.

What we do not use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies. We do not run ad pixels, conversion tags or remarketing lists, and Consent Mode's advertising signals stay denied permanently.
  • No social media trackers. Our social links are ordinary links, not embedded widgets.
  • No profiling or automated decision-making, and no sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • No third-party fonts. The site uses the fonts already on your device, so nothing is requested from a font CDN.

Third-party services that set no cookies

For completeness, two services receive a request from your browser without storing anything on your device. Both necessarily see your IP address, as any server you connect to does:

  • images.unsplash.com — serves some of the photography on this site.
  • api.web3forms.com — receives the contact form when, and only when, you submit it. What you type in that form is covered by our Privacy Policy, not by this one.

Browser-level controls

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser, which overrides anything we do. Note that deleting az_consent makes the banner reappear, because your answer is what that cookie was holding.

Browser "Do Not Track" headers are not covered by an agreed standard and most sites ignore them, so we do not rely on that signal. Instead, we keep analytics off for everyone by default, which achieves the same outcome without requiring you to trust a header.

5. Legal Basis

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland you have the rights set out in Section 6 of our Privacy Policy, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, plus the right to complain to your national data protection authority. California residents have the parallel rights described in the same section.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we add, remove or repurpose a cookie, we will update the table above, change the "Last updated" date and increment the consent version. Increasing the version invalidates previously stored answers, so you will be asked again rather than being held to a choice you made about a different set of cookies.
