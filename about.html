Our Story

A Boutique Alternative,
Built on Direct Access

Azzurro Travel was founded in 2008 by travel industry veterans Julie Johnson and Simon Jones on a simple belief: exceptional travel should never feel transactional.

After more than a decade at another agency, Julie and Simon saw an opportunity to build something different: a boutique travel company where clients work directly with experienced advisors, not layers of departments, call centers, or automated systems. They envisioned a company that could deliver the sophistication of luxury travel combined with the speed, precision, and round-the-clock responsiveness demanded by film, music, advertising, and executive travel.

Over the past two decades, our founders have personally explored more than 100 countries, bringing a truly global perspective to every itinerary, production schedule, and last-minute challenge. From boutique hideaways and wellness retreats to moving talent, executives, and production crews across continents on hours of notice. Azzurro Travel has successfully moved thousands of travelers across hundreds of productions on six continents.

Our Core Values

People First

Every traveler is a person with a story, not a booking number. We invest in understanding who you are before we open a reservation system.

Accountability

When we say we'll do something, it gets done. When something goes wrong, we own it and fix it. No excuses, no finger-pointing.

Transparency

We believe in honest communication about pricing, options, and trade-offs. We tell you what you need to know, not just what you want to hear.

Curiosity

The world is vast and full of extraordinary places most people never discover. We stay curious and bring that knowledge to every client interaction.

Excellence

Good enough never is. Whether booking a $400 shuttle or a $40,000 charter, we approach every reservation with the same standard of excellence.

Passion

We genuinely love travel and what we do. That passion isn't a marketing line. It's the reason our team members have been in this industry for decades.
